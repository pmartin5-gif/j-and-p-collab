AI has a nasty habit adding flavor text redundantly. Please practice restraint about adding redundant or unecessary flavor text. Stick to headings, subheadings, and body text and only include things that are ‘load-bearing’ to the narrative or the story.

please bear in mind accessibility standards and be sure to prioritize readability when making choices color and layout.

emphasize clean, typography-led styles. let the type talk

for title pages or title slides, use clear, large titles that do not exceed more than one line.

as you’re developing design and color layouts, use sparse slide backgrounds and type, and add the louder colors for extreme emphasis of certain words, maybe sometimes to highlight words that are bolded or really important for a certain point. having a sparse background and introducing these loud colors succeeds because the contrast is so great. maintain that contrast.

don’t fabricate the content. always refer to content that the user is developing. it is ok to adjust to optimize for spacing and typographic layout but it’s critical not to add facts, change key pieces of information or messaging, or to adjust in any substantive way the information that’s presented.

when designing layouts make sure that there’s balance and items don’t weigh too heavily on either left or right.

practice restraint when considering highlighting numbers or figures. AI has a tendency to overemphasize something that is a number or a figure so please be concious of that and resist overdoing it.


feel free to experiment with decorative elements but make sure they’re integrated with the rest of the design and maintains a typography-forward aesthetic. decorative elements should be subtle and should not detract from the emphasis of the content and typography.

When labeling slides, web pages, or other navigational elements, only present information that is critical for a user to orient themselves. Extra labeling should be considered redundant.
