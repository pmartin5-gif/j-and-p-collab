Visual learnings

Decision pathing

Much of our analysis centered on decision-pathing to understand why someone might choose to go to farmer’s market over the other options.

From the interviews, we assume that shoppers are buying food to cook at home.

They make their decisions based on

  • Advantages of farmer’s markets
  • Disadvantages of farmer’s markets
  • Advantages of alternatives (supermarkets)

Visual learnings

Wins on Experience

  • The reasons that people choose farmer’s markets over supermarkets are varied and can have either a pragmatic or experiential basis.
  • Supermarkets can compete with farmer’s markets on pragmatic reasons, like pricing and variety.
  • However, interviewees frequently cited experiential motivation to choose farmer’s markets, which cannot be matched by supermarkets.

Insights

01

Common Intentions

For most people, the ‘job to be done’ is to find ingredients to cook at home.

02

Stiff competition

The direct alternative to a farmer’s market is a supermarket. The supermarket clearly outcompetes farmer’s market on the availability of packaged goods, consistency, and shelter from the weather. For other practical concerns like variety, quality, price, or convenience, any advantage over farmer’s markets is conditional.

03

Clear advantage is sentimental

While people frequent farmer’s markets for pragmatic reasons, they also frequently cited experiential or sentimental reasons – nostalgia, comfort, community, and supporting local farmers. Alternatives like supermarkets don’t compete with farmers markets on these criteria.

Recommendations

01

Mitigate Practical Issues

Farmers markets are sometimes out-competed by alternatives in practical areas.

There may be adjustments that can mitigate some of these issues to make farmers markets a more dependable, compelling option.

02

Maximize Experience

However, not all buying decisions are practical.

Farmers Markets have a clear advantage over alternatives for those seeking an enriching experience.

Find ways to ‘lean in’ and maximize the potential for customers to connect with the market and each other.

Recommendations

Mitigate Practical Issues

Offer vendors electrical access so they can serve ice cream on hot days.

Establish a ‘flow’ of pedestrian traffic.

Show which vendors are present and what’s available.

Offer ponchos or umbrellas during the rain.

Recommendations

Maximize Experience

Provide more background info on vendors and their farms.

In-person training on how to choose the best produce.

Public seating to create a ‘third space.’

Serve booze.
